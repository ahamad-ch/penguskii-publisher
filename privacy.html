Privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Penguskii Publisher is operated by the owner of the Penguskii channels. It is a private tool for publishing the owner’s approved videos. This policy covers this informational website and the publishing workflow. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact penguskiitech@gmail.com.

Information the tool accesses

When the owner connects an account, the tool receives authorization tokens from Google or Meta. It does not request or store Google or Instagram account passwords.

  • YouTube: channel ID, the uploads playlist, recent video IDs, titles, descriptions, publication times, visibility and processing status. YouTube permissions allow uploading videos and reading account information needed to confirm the channel and publication.
  • Instagram: account ID and username, recent media IDs and publication times, upload container status, captions, media type and published post links.
  • Publishing material: owner-supplied video files, scripts, titles, descriptions, captions, publishing instructions, file hashes, timestamps and publication records.

How information is used

The workflow uses this information to verify the connected accounts, upload approved content, confirm publication, prevent duplicate uploads, enforce the owner’s publishing limits and diagnose failures. It does not read direct messages, manage comments or collect viewer profiles.

Account data is not sold, used for advertising profiles or used to train general-purpose AI models. Penguskii Publisher’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and service providers

The workflow runs on the owner’s computer and, when enabled, GitHub Actions. Authorization credentials are stored in local configuration or GitHub Actions secrets. Access tokens are used during execution. Private publication records, including post IDs, links, hashes and status, are stored in the owner’s private project repository and publication ledger.

Videos and metadata are sent to Google/YouTube or Meta/Instagram for publication. Upload-ready video files may be hosted at public Supabase storage URLs so that the platforms can retrieve them; anyone with one of those URLs can access that video. GitHub processes workflow code, credentials, logs and build artifacts as part of running the service. Access is limited to the owner, service providers and people the owner authorizes to operate or support the workflow.

This public website contains app information and policies, not account credentials or private publication records. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site. The pages do not add analytics, advertising, cookies or embedded social players. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

Penguskii Publisher uses YouTube API Services. See the Google Privacy Policy, Meta Privacy Policy, GitHub Privacy Statement and Supabase Privacy Policy for those providers’ practices.

Retention and deletion

Credentials are retained while the account connection is in use, until removed or revoked. Publication records are retained to support the owner’s publishing history and duplicate prevention. Stored records and media are removed manually; there is no automatic account-deletion feature on this website.

To request deletion, email penguskiitech@gmail.com identifying the connected channel or account. Do not send passwords or tokens. The operator will verify the request, stop the affected publishing workflow, and remove credentials and associated stored account data from active storage. Requests are handled as soon as possible, with a target of seven calendar days after verification. Repository history, logs and artifacts are included in the deletion review; provider-managed backup retention is governed by the provider.

Deleting workflow data does not delete videos already published on YouTube or Instagram. The account owner can manage those posts directly on each platform.

Revoke access

You can revoke Google authorization at any time through Google Account permissions. Instagram access can be removed through the account’s Apps and websites settings. Revocation prevents future authorized API access; contact the address above to request removal of information already stored by the tool.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the workflow’s data practices change. The effective date appears at the top. Questions, concerns and privacy requests can be sent to penguskiitech@gmail.com.
