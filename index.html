Penguskii makes short videos with one useful trick, tool or idea. Penguskii Publisher is the private tool that helps the account owner bring them to YouTube and Instagram.

Meet the publishing tool ↓

One owner.
Two channels.
A little less busywork.

Penguskii Publisher uploads owner-approved videos and their titles, descriptions and captions to the owner’s connected YouTube channel and Instagram professional account. It checks the account identity and publication status, and keeps records to help prevent duplicate posts.

It uses YouTube API Services and the Instagram API. The owner grants access directly through the platforms’ authorization processes. YouTube access is used to upload videos and read channel and video information needed to verify publication.

The tool is for the Penguskii account owner only. This website provides app information and policies; it does not offer public registration or account connection.
