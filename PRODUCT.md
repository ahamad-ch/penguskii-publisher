# Penguskii Publisher website

## Register
brand

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
The Penguskii account owner and visitors reviewing the publishing tool's identity and data practices, including Google OAuth reviewers. The site provides a public homepage, contact, privacy policy and terms; it does not provide sign-in or public onboarding.

## Brand
Friendly, curious, plainspoken. Preserve the existing penguin artwork, purple and yellow brand palette, and bold Montserrat headline typography. A visitor on a phone in daylight needs to understand the tool and read its policy without distraction: bright white reading areas and the existing purple brand hero.

## Principles
Explain actual functionality and data flow; avoid unsupported automation or security claims. Keep disclosures accessible from every page. No analytics, trackers, remote fonts or embeds. Semantic HTML, visible focus, responsive text and reduced-motion support.
